# Iran Energy News Analysis

## Overview
This report analyzes the impact of the ongoing conflict in Iran on the global energy sector.

## Key Findings
- **Market Volatility:** The war has resulted in significant fluctuations in crude oil prices.
- **Shifts to Renewables:** Countries are accelerating their transition to renewable energy to reduce dependency on Iranian oil.
- **Inflationary Pressures:** Increased energy prices are contributing to broader inflation globally.

## Facts vs. Opinions
- **Facts:** Data on oil price changes from the past month.
- **Opinions:** Expert analyses predicting long-term shifts in energy policies.
